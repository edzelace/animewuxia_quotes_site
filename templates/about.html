About This Site

This site is a tribute to less-real.com, a once-beloved website where fans could explore, collect, and share memorable quotes from anime. Although less-real has been offline for a long time, its spirit lives on in Excel archives preserved by the community.

With this modern revival, our goal is to bring that nostalgic experience back—preserving legendary quotes and introducing new ones from the latest anime, webtoons, light novels, and even Chinese webnovels like wuxia and xianxia.

Whether you're reminiscing about your favorite characters or discovering profound lines from unfamiliar series, we hope this site helps you reconnect with the emotions, inspiration, and wisdom that only stories can offer.

You can explore random quotes, search by anime, character, or keyword, and contribute to expanding the library for future fans.

This is a fan-made revival. All quotes belong to their respective creators and publishers.
